# Definitions and explanations

*Decided by the owner in the founding prompt, 2026-09-16. Binding: a session may not reopen it; it may queue a question in [open-questions](../open-questions.md) with a working assumption. Charter: [PROJECT.md](../../PROJECT.md).*

**Ruling.** A traditional phrasal definition is the primary field. An optional plain-prose explanation field exists for words that need it (function words, discourse markers, words whose use is easier to explain than to define). The two are separate fields so a later adaptation can choose either.

**Consequence.** `senses[].definition` is required and phrasal; `senses[].explanation` is null unless the style guide's test for it is met.
