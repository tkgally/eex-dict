# What gets external verification

*Decided by the owner in the founding prompt, 2026-09-16. Binding: a session may not reopen it; it may queue a question in [open-questions](../open-questions.md) with a working assumption. Charter: [PROJECT.md](../../PROJECT.md).*

**Ruling.** Pronunciation and inflections are checked by multi-model agreement and, where a run can reach one, by a run-time consultation of an open resource that leaves nothing in the repository. Frequency bands and regional labels are checked by multi-model agreement. Definitions, examples, grammar, labels, collocations, notes, and adaptation notes rely on multi-model review that reads every field, including notes. The session reads every blocking issue itself and decides; it never applies a reviewer's fix blindly and never rejects one without a logged reason. A reviewer disagreement on a factual question is resolved by open data if any bears on it, otherwise the entry carries a flag and the question goes to `reviews/needs_curator.txt`.

**Consequence.** The pipeline order in `CLAUDE.md`; the decision ledger `reviews/decisions.jsonl`.
