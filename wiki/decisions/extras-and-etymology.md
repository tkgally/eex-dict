# Extras and the etymology rule

*Decided by the owner in the founding prompt, 2026-09-16. Binding: a session may not reopen it; it may queue a question in [open-questions](../open-questions.md) with a working assumption. Charter: [PROJECT.md](../../PROJECT.md).*

**Ruling.** A collocation box, a word-family box, synonym-discrimination notes, and common learner errors, on every entry where they apply. Etymology only when verified against two open sources **and** relevant to understanding the word as used today (Greek and Latin roots, transparent compounds, borrowings that explain a spelling or a sense); otherwise omitted.

**Consequence.** `etymology` is null or `{text, sources_consulted (two or more), checked}`; the reviewers may flag `etymology` as blocking when it fails either test.
