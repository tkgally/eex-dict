# The website

*Decided by the owner in the founding prompt, 2026-09-16. Binding: a session may not reopen it; it may queue a question in [open-questions](../open-questions.md) with a working assumption. Charter: [PROJECT.md](../../PROJECT.md).*

**Ruling.** Static, mobile-first, no framework, built by CI into `docs/` and never committed; an AI-authorship disclosure on every page; search that understands inflected forms; browse, random, recent; hover or tap previews on every linked word; frequency bands displayed; adaptation fields behind a "translator's view" toggle.

**Why a Routine mode.** In the earlier project no scheduled mode touched the site, so English search returned nothing and links were off by default for months. Here `site` is a Routine mode that builds `docs/` locally and checks the home page and three entry pages in a real browser at phone width.
