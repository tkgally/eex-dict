# Headword selection without external lists

*Decided by the owner in the founding prompt, 2026-09-16. Binding: a session may not reopen it; it may queue a question in [open-questions](../open-questions.md) with a working assumption. Charter: [PROJECT.md](../../PROJECT.md).*

**Ruling.** No external frequency data and no external word list is consulted, for anything. The defining vocabulary, the headword queue, and the frequency bands come from the models' own judgment of how basic, common, and important a word is, gathered from several models and settled editorially by the session that builds the list. Selection is editorial and the site says so (`about.html`).

**Method.** Recorded in [defining-vocabulary](defining-vocabulary.md) once the list is built: three models from different labs propose words independently, in chunks by semantic field and part of speech; the intersection is the core; the session adjudicates disagreements and fills gaps; bands 2 and 3 are estimated by the same models and settled by majority.
