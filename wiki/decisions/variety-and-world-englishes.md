# Variety of English and World Englishes

*Decided by the owner in the founding prompt, 2026-09-16. Binding: a session may not reopen it; it may queue a question in [open-questions](../open-questions.md) with a working assumption. Charter: [PROJECT.md](../../PROJECT.md).*

**Ruling.** American English is primary for spelling, pronunciation, and examples. British spelling, pronunciation, and usage are recorded in structured fields (`variants`, `pronunciation.british`, region labels), never mixed into prose. In a later phase, beyond the basic vocabulary, the dictionary actively seeks vocabulary and grammar from World Englishes (Indian, Singaporean, Nigerian, Philippine, Caribbean, and others) and from forms of English evolving online without strong ties to a region.

**Consequence.** The region label set in `schema/vocabularies.json` is open-ended and starts with sixteen values including `online`; the headword queue has a `world` source. Adding a region is a logged decision. Examples are written in American spelling; a British-only sense carries the `British` label and, where the spelling differs, a variant.
