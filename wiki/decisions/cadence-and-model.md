# Cadence and model independence

*Decided by the owner in the founding prompt, 2026-09-16. Binding: a session may not reopen it; it may queue a question in [open-questions](../open-questions.md) with a working assumption. Charter: [PROJECT.md](../../PROJECT.md).*

**Ruling.** One to six Routine runs per day; runs may overlap. The model and the cadence may change later; nothing in the repository may depend on either.

**Consequence.** Claims ([conventions](../conventions.md) section 3) make overlapping runs safe; the selector reads the budget ledger, not a run count per day; the drafter is a role in `config/models.md`.
