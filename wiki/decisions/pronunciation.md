# Pronunciation

*Decided by the owner in the founding prompt, 2026-09-16. Binding: a session may not reopen it; it may queue a question in [open-questions](../open-questions.md) with a working assumption. Charter: [PROJECT.md](../../PROJECT.md).*

**Ruling.** IPA in General American with stress and syllable breaks, and a British transcription, both at launch. An audio slot left empty; text-to-speech audio is on the roadmap. Source: model knowledge, tested in the founding session and thereafter checked by agreement between models. Open pronouncing resources (the CMU Pronouncing Dictionary for American, Wiktionary for British) may be consulted at run time to check a transcription, but nothing from them is stored in the repository; an entry records only that a check was made and what it found.

**Consequence.** The agreement rule and the panel are fixed by the experiment recorded in [pronunciation-pipeline](pronunciation-pipeline.md) and implemented in `tools/pronounce_check.py`.
