# Senses: order, splitting, core idea

*Decided by the owner in the founding prompt, 2026-09-16. Binding: a session may not reopen it; it may queue a question in [open-questions](../open-questions.md) with a working assumption. Charter: [PROJECT.md](../../PROJECT.md).*

**Ruling.** Senses are ordered most-useful-first. A written splitting principle keeps the model from over-splitting: a new sense only when learners need a different definition, grammar, collocation set, or translation. Sub-senses are allowed. Polysemous entries open with a short core-idea line.

**Consequence.** The style guide carries the principle and the tests; `validate.py` warns when an entry with three or more senses has no core idea and errors when a one-sense entry has one.
