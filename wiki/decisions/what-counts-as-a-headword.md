# What counts as a headword

*Decided by the owner in the founding prompt, 2026-09-16. Binding: a session may not reopen it; it may queue a question in [open-questions](../open-questions.md) with a working assumption. Charter: [PROJECT.md](../../PROJECT.md).*

**Ruling.** Phrasal verbs get their own entries (`give-up-phrv`). Idioms and fixed phrases are addressable sub-entries under a keyword entry (`bank-n#break-the-bank`). Affixes (`un-prefix`, `-ness-suffix`) and common abbreviations (`asap-abbr`) are included. Proper nouns only when lexicalized (`google-v`). Derived words get their own entries when their meaning is not predictable from the base; otherwise they appear in the base word's word-family box.

**Keyword for a phrase.** The first noun in the phrase, else the first verb, else the first content word; the style guide gives the details and the exceptions.
