# The adaptation layer

*Decided by the owner in the founding prompt, 2026-09-16. Binding: a session may not reopen it; it may queue a question in [open-questions](../open-questions.md) with a working assumption. Charter: [PROJECT.md](../../PROJECT.md).*

**Ruling.** Every entry carries brief language-neutral notes of five kinds: semantic splits common across languages; grammar traps such as countability or argument structure; cultural or encyclopedic background; false-friend risks for internationalisms; pronunciation traps. Plus empty per-language slots (`l1`) for equivalents and notes. After milestone 1, Japanese is seeded on about 200 entries to validate the schema.

**Consequence.** `adaptation` objects at entry, sense, and phrase level, each field at most 60 words and nullable; `l1` keyed by BCP-47 code, shape documented in [conventions](../conventions.md); shown on the site only in the translator's view.
