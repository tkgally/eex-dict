# The defining vocabulary and the frequency bands: method and counts

*To be completed by the founding session's Stage B (2026-09-16). Until then this page records only the method the charter fixes.*

**Method (fixed by the owner).** No external frequency data and no external word list. Three models from different labs, independently and in chunks by semantic field and part of speech, propose the words a learner's dictionary needs in order to define everything else; the intersection is the core; the session adjudicates the disagreements and fills obvious gaps (function words, light verbs, the words needed to define the words). Then bands 2 and 3 are estimated by the same three models for candidate headwords and settled by majority. Model roles are `wordlist-1`, `wordlist-2`, `wordlist-3` in `config/models.md`.
