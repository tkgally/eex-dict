# Reader level

*Decided by the owner in the founding prompt, 2026-09-16. Binding: a session may not reopen it; it may queue a question in [open-questions](../open-questions.md) with a working assumption. Charter: [PROJECT.md](../../PROJECT.md).*

**Ruling.** The reader is an intermediate or advanced learner of English, roughly CEFR B1 to C2. Entries are complete enough for advanced learners; definitions are plain enough for B1 readers.

**Consequence.** The definition field is written in defining-vocabulary English (see [defining-vocabulary-rule](defining-vocabulary-rule.md)); completeness comes from senses, phrases, collocations, and notes, not from harder definitions. No CEFR levels are shown on entries (see [frequency-bands](frequency-bands.md)).
