# Entry unit and homographs

*Decided by the owner in the founding prompt, 2026-09-16. Binding: a session may not reopen it; it may queue a question in [open-questions](../open-questions.md) with a working assumption. Charter: [PROJECT.md](../../PROJECT.md).*

**Ruling.** One entry per headword plus part of speech. Same-spelling, same-part-of-speech homographs get separate entries only when unrelated in meaning and origin (`bat-n`, `bat-n-2`); related meanings are senses of one entry. A word-family box ties related entries together.

**Consequence.** The slug carries the part of speech; a second homograph takes `-2` in creation order, never reassigned ([slugs-and-ids](slugs-and-ids.md)).
