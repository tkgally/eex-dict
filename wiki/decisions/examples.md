# Examples

*Decided by the owner in the founding prompt, 2026-09-16. Binding: a session may not reopen it; it may queue a question in [open-questions](../open-questions.md) with a working assumption. Charter: [PROJECT.md](../../PROJECT.md).*

**Ruling.** Two to four examples per sense, written by the model, carrying typical collocations and grammar patterns, with **no checkable real-world facts** (dates, statistics, named events, science claims: they are a hallucination sink). Settings culturally neutral. Personal names allowed but short and used across cultures (Kim, Mari, Sam, Ana, Lee, Omar, Yuki, Ravi, Sara); avoid names with strong American or British associations (Wayne, Clive, Bubba, Nigel). No brand names.

**Consequence.** `validate.py` enforces the count; the reviewers' checklist has an `example-policy` family for facts, brands, and names; the style guide lists the allowed names.
