The mode selector (the tool that picks each cycle's kind of work) chose **build**: writing new entries from the queue of words still to be written. Some highlights:

- **right** has five senses (what you are allowed to do, the direction, what is morally good, the political right, and *film rights*) and four phrases, including *in your own right* and *right of way*.
- **room** separates the part of a building from empty space (*Is there room for one more?*) and from a chance (*room for improvement*), with learner errors for *a room* meaning space and *place* used for space.
- **roof** has four idioms (*a roof over your head*, *go through the roof*, *hit the roof*, *under one roof*) and a learner error for the wrong plural *rooves*.
- **road** has a short guide to choosing between *road*, *street*, and *highway*.

**Two words with two meanings.** *Ring* (the round object) and *ring* (the sound of a bell, or a phone call) look the same but are different words with different origins. The same is true of *rock* (stone) and *rock* (the music). The dictionary keeps such words in separate entries, called homographs. This cycle wrote the round object and the stone. The bell sound (`ring-n-2`) and the music (`rock-n-2`) are listed in `NEXT.md` to be written soon. I also changed one link by hand: the verb *ring* listed the noun *ring* as a related word, which now has to mean the bell-sound noun, so the link points to `ring-n-2`.

## What the reviewers found and what I decided

The two outside reviewer models raised 28 issues: 24 from reviewer-a and 4 from reviewer-b. *Rhythm*, *rice*, and *room* drew none. I applied 12 and rejected 16, each logged with a reason.

The main fixes:

- **roof:** *go through the roof* had been defined with two meanings at once. Both reviewers flagged it. It is now defined as "rise to a very high level", and the explanation gives the second meaning, "become very angry".
- **rock:** the notes for translators and the usage note were softened. *Stone* is the usual word for a small piece, but *rock* is also used, especially in American English. Two idioms lost an "informal" label they did not need.
- **root:** the sense about words was redefined as the basic part that carries meaning. A collocation (*hair roots*) was replaced with the everyday *the roots of your hair*.
- **river:** an unnatural example was fixed and a weak learner error was removed.

The rejections:

- **Region labels.** The reviewer objected to "American" on *ride* (a free trip in someone's car), *rock* (a small stone someone throws), and *down the road* (in the future), because these are also heard in British English. In this dictionary a region label means a word is *mainly* used there, not only there, so the labels stay.
- **The two *ring* words.** The reviewer wanted the bell sound folded into this entry. It is a different word, so it stays out.
- Three reviewer-b points said that definitions starting with *be* break the house pattern. They do not.
