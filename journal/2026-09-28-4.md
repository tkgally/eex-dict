# 2026-09-28 (4) — Lint: the nineteenth housekeeping pass

## What this run did

This was the fourth and last cycle of the scheduled run that began at 00:44 UTC. A "cycle" is one unit of work that is checked, recorded, and merged on its own. The first three cycles were reviews and merged as pull requests #110, #111, and #112. Five runs had gone by since the last lint pass, so the mode selector (`tools/next_mode.py`) forced this one.

A lint pass is housekeeping. Scripts check the whole dictionary and its notes. Then I read their output and the project's own pages for problems a script cannot judge. This pass found nothing that needed repair.

## What was checked, and what it found

- **Word caps and links.** Every page is within its word limit, and every relative link resolves.
- **Cross-references.** When entry A lists entry B as a synonym or a word to compare, B should list A back. `tools/crossref.py` adds those mirror links, but it guesses the wrong sense of B about one time in five, so each new one is checked by hand. This pass it wrote none. The 1,794 targets it reports as missing are words named in cross-references that have no entry yet; they are already in the queue of words to write.
- **Vocabulary.** A second script checks every definition against the defining vocabulary, the list of plain words definitions must use. It found no violations.
- **Stale claims.** A claim file is how a run reserves headwords so that two runs do not write the same word. There were no stale ones.
- **Reviewer precision.** Each finding from the two reviewer models is either applied or rejected, and precision is the share applied. When a reviewer falls below 30 percent on one kind of finding over twenty or more decisions, it stops being asked about that kind. No new kind crossed the line; the five switched off earlier stay off. One is about to cross: the first reviewer's findings on inflections (the forms of a word, such as *ran* for *run*). It has had 1 of 19 accepted, so one more rejection will switch it off at the next lint pass. The figures are in `wiki/notes/reviewer-precision.md`.
- **Index, open questions, and the curator list.** The wiki index lists every page. The open questions still carry their working assumptions. `reviews/needs_curator.txt`, the list of items for you, has no duplicate lines.
