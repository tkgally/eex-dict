## What this cycle did

A **lint cycle** is the dictionary's housekeeping pass. It makes no new entries and no paid calls. It runs the mechanical checks over everything, then a judgmental read of the knowledge base (the `wiki/` pages that hold the project's rules and notes) for stale or contradictory statements. The scheduler (`tools/next_mode.py`) forced a lint because five cycles had passed since the last one.

The mechanical checks:

- **Size caps** (`tools/check_caps.py`): all respected.
- **Links** between the project's pages (`tools/check_links.py`): all 493 pages resolve.
- **Cross-references** (`tools/crossref.py --all --apply`): when entry A names entry B as a synonym, opposite, or family member, B should name A back. This added five such return links on three entries. I checked each by hand, as the rules require, because the tool sometimes attaches a link to the wrong sense. All five were right this time. *friendly* gained *befriend* in its word family, and *cold* and *distant* as opposites of its *kind and pleasant* sense. *fine* gained *good* on its *good quality* sense. *former* gained *old* on its *before now* sense.
- **Defining vocabulary** (`tools/lint_vocab.py --all --queue`): the rule is that every word in a definition is either in the defining word list or has its own entry. It found no violations in all 2,098 entries and queued nothing new.
- **Claim files** (`tools/claim.py --prune`): three old claim files, the records of which words a past cycle had taken, were removed. All their words are done.
