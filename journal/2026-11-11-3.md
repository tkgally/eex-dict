**What the entries contain.** A few points worth knowing:

- *login* has two senses: the process of getting into a system (*after three failed logins*), and the name and password you use. Its learner error is the very common *I can't login*: the verb is two words, *log in*.
- *loser* separates the plain competition sense from the insult (*a total loser*), which is labeled informal and disapproving. It also has a subsense for *a good loser* and *a sore loser*.
- *luggage* carries two real learner errors, *three luggages* and *a new luggage*. Both come from the noun being uncountable.
- *maintenance* has three senses: repair work (with a subsense for the staff who do it, *call maintenance*), keeping something at a level (formal), and the British sense of money paid after a divorce. A pronunciation-and-spelling note warns that it is spelled *-ten-*, not *-tain-* as in *maintain*, and that the stress moves to the first part.
- *making* holds *the makings of* (*the makings of a fine player*) and three idioms: *in the making*, *be the making of someone*, and *of your own making*.
- *male* (the noun) mirrors the existing *female* entry, with the same usage note: as a word for people it belongs to science, police reports, and forms, and in conversation it can sound cold.

**What the reviewers found and what I decided.** Each entry goes to two reviewer models from different companies, which check every field and mark each problem as blocking (must be settled before publishing) or minor. They raised 37 issues, 7 of them blocking. I accepted 30 and rejected 7, and every decision is logged with a reason.

- Both reviewers rightly objected that *password* was listed as a synonym of *login*. It is only part of a login, so it is now a "compare" word with a short note.
- Both also saw that *right after login* uses the noun without *a* or *the*, while the grammar said it was countable only. That sense is now marked "countable or uncountable".
- They were also right on several smaller points. *Hand luggage* is a noun + noun pairing, not adjective + noun. *Trick* and *repair* are related words, not synonyms. *Under new management* belongs to the "people in charge" sense. An example about soup "working its magic" did not fit the definition. The definition of *make* used the word *made*, which is circular, so it now says *produced*.
- I rejected three blocking flags on *making*. They said the entry claims "no plural" while one sense is used only as *makings*. The reviewers are right that this looks inconsistent, but the inflections field is written by a script (`tools/inflect.py`), never by hand. The script cannot record a plural for a noun whose other sense is uncountable. This is a known gap, already recorded in `wiki/notes/inflect-uncountable-plural-gap.md` from *money*, *finance*, *energy*, and *rain*, and *making* is now added to that list. The examples mark **makings** by hand, as the house rules require.
- I also rejected a request to add a register label to *male* for people. The usage note already says where the word is used, and the existing *female* entry does the same.
