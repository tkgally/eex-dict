## What the reviewers found and what I decided

The panel raised only 8 issues, 5 of them blocking (a blocking issue must be settled before an entry counts as reviewed). I applied 5 and rejected 3.

What I accepted:

- The second sense of *origin* was defined as "the country, race, or social class" a family comes from. It now reads "the country, people, or social group". I had first written "culture", but that word is outside the defining vocabulary, the list of plain words definitions must use.
- The photo-album sense said "plain pages", but many albums have pockets or mounts.
- *cough mixture* need not be several substances mixed together; the definition is now "a liquid medicine, especially one for a cough".
- One example (*an aircraft engineer*) did not really show the noun at work, and one collocation for *origin* was not typical.

What I rejected:

- Two pronunciation objections, on *origin* (British) and *responsibility* (American). In both, the reviewer's "correct" version was the same as ours. The pronunciation check had verified both.
- A request to remove *math* and *maths* from the variants of *mathematics*. The house schema lets a variant be a short "form", and listing them lets site search find *mathematics* from *math*. I did remove a duplicate "see also" link to *math* that I had added myself.

The cross-reference tool made no changes. It queued *mix* (noun) as a future entry, because *mixture* now compares itself with it.
