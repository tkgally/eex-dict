# 2026-09-26 (thirty-second run): six verbs, *sense* to *share*

## What this run did

This was the thirteenth cycle of this session, and a **build** run. A build run drafts new entries from the queue, the ordered list of words still to be written. The previous build merged as pull request #100. The run clock (the script that decides whether a new cycle may start) still allowed one more cycle. This one took six words instead of eight so that it would finish comfortably inside the time limit.

The six verbs, all from band 1 (the most basic words), were:

- **sense**: becoming aware of something without being told (*She sensed that something was wrong*), and machines that sense movement.
- **sentence**: giving someone a punishment in court. A learner error covers *sentenced for five years*: the time comes after *to*, and *for* introduces the crime.
- **serve**: six senses. They are serving food, serving customers, serving in the army or as mayor, serving as a table, serving in tennis, and serving a prison sentence. There is also the phrase *serve someone right*.
- **set**: seven senses. They are putting something down, setting a date or a price, setting an alarm, the sun setting, setting someone free, a film set in a village, and glue or jelly setting. A learner error covers *setted*.
- **shape**: giving something a form, and influencing how something develops (*His childhood shaped the person he became*).
- **share**: using something together, dividing it, giving part of it, telling others (*share the news*), and having the same feeling. A learner error covers *share me your notes*.

## Checks and reviews

Every entry went through the full pipeline, the fixed sequence of checks each entry must pass:

- **Format and inflections.** All six passed. *Set* took its forms (*set*, *setting*) from the table of irregular forms.
- **Defining vocabulary.** Three words in definitions were outside the defining vocabulary, the list of about 2,400 basic words that definitions are written in: *tennis*, *deserves*, and *net*. They were reworded.
- **Pronunciation.** All twelve transcriptions were confirmed by the panel of pronunciation models.
- **Reviewers.** The two reviewer models raised 13 issues, the fewest of any cycle this session. Each has a logged decision: 7 applied and 6 rejected.

The main fixes:

- *Serve* in sport was "hitting the ball over the net to the other player". That does not fit games like squash. It is now "start playing a point in a ball game by hitting the ball first", and the sense can take an object (*serve the ball*). The prison sense now covers other punishments too.
- *Set an example* moved from "cause a state" to "decide", where it fits better.
- *Shape* in its first sense was defined with the noun *shape*, and it now uses *form*.
- The translator's note on *share* said the other person "never" comes straight after the verb. It now makes that claim only for the sense *let someone have part of something*.

Rejected: four requests to drop explanations such as "Used about the sun and the moon". The style guide says a restriction on the subject belongs in exactly that field. Two objections to grammar values that are in the dictionary's closed list of patterns were also rejected.

The cross-reference tool added back-links, which are links pointing back from an existing entry to a new one. Two were on the wrong sense:

- *sense* on *feel*'s "have an emotion" sense, moved to "experience a feeling in your body or mind"
- *set* on *place*'s "put someone in a situation" sense, moved to "put something somewhere"

The second run of the tool then added one correct link from *seem* to *feel*.
