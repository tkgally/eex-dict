# 2026-10-20, cycle 3 of the 12:44 UTC run: second review of *rubber* to *search*

## What this cycle did

This was a **review** cycle: a second reading of entries that had been through the two outside reviewers (the review panel, two language models from other companies) only once. The scheduler (`tools/next_mode.py`) chose it as the most overdue kind of work. The block is always the oldest such entries; this time twenty nouns drafted on 2026-10-07: *rubber, rug, ruin, safety, sail, salary, sale, salt, sand, satisfaction, sauce, scale, scene, schedule, school, science, score, screen, sea, search*.

Each entry went to both reviewers again. I read every issue against the entry and either changed the entry (*applied*) or left it as it was (*rejected*, with a logged reason in `reviews/decisions.jsonl`). Nothing was escalated to the owner.

## What the reviewers found and what I decided

Three entries drew no issues (*ruin, sail, sale*). There were 39 issues in all; I applied 24 and rejected 15.

Applied:

- ***school*** and ***sea***: the opening summary sentence (the *core idea*) left out senses. *School* now covers universities and places for learning a skill; *sea* now says *a very large amount of something* rather than *anything that seems as large*.
- Two synonym links were really contrasts. A school inside a university is not the same as a department, and *art* is the opposite of *science* in *an art, not a science*. Both pairs moved from *synonyms* to *compare*, on both entries, with short notes.
- ***sea***: the note that Americans say *ocean* where the British say *sea* was too absolute and appeared twice; it is now softened (*often*, *usually*) and kept once.
- ***scene***: *set the scene* now means give people the information they need to understand what happens next, or create the conditions for something. The learner-error note no longer treats *view* as the same as *scenery*.
- ***score***: a learner error (*What is the point?* for *What's the score?*) was doubtful and was removed.
- ***rubber*** (the condom sense), ***rug*** (*cut a rug* is now labeled *dated*, and an example with an unclear *it* was rewritten), ***safety*** and ***satisfaction*** (two translator's notes that said *no plural* or *only countable* too absolutely), ***screen*** (the *the screen* subsense: *movies and television, as a kind of art or as a business*), ***search*** (tool names such as *search engine* replaced in the collocations), ***scale*** (a small wording fix).

Rejected, with the main reasons:

- ***sea***: reviewer-a said *old-fashioned* is not a currency label. It is one, in the closed list of labels the dictionary uses.
- ***sand***: reviewer-b said the forms line "no plural" contradicts the notes about *the sands*. That line is written by a script (`tools/inflect.py`) and has a known gap for one plural-only use; the notes are right.
- ***salt***: the chemistry sense, *formed when an acid joins with a base*, is the standard plain account.
- ***salary***: the learner error about an hourly rate stands; its note already says *not usually*.
- Smaller: *built on sand* is the usual form of the idiom; *according to schedule*, *a search warrant* are typical; *scores of* and *on that score* need no register label.
