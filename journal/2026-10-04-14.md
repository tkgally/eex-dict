- *Worry* mentioned a rare British use (a dog that worries sheep) inside the explanation of another sense. I removed it rather than give it a sense of its own.

- *Worry* and *sorry* do not rhyme in American English either, so the pronunciation note stays.
- *Amount* is itself a countable noun (*an amount*, *large amounts*), so its grammar is right.
- The everyday sense of *animal* that excludes birds and fish (*birds and animals*) stays as a sense, because the entry already explains that it is a loose use.
