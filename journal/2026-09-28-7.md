This was the third cycle of the scheduled run that began at 06:44 UTC (a "cycle" is one unit of work, merged on its own).

The block had thirteen entries in two groups:

I read each entry before the panel saw it. What I added or fixed:

- **adopt** listed *borrow* as a synonym of *take a child into your family*, which is wrong. It now sits under the *start using an idea or method* sense, with a note. I also added a subsense for adopting a pet, a new sense for accepting a plan or a law officially (*The committee voted to adopt the proposal*), and a learner error for the very common mix-up with **adapt**.
- **adult**: a new sense, *acting in a sensible, calm way* (*Let's be adult about this*), and a pronunciation note saying both *AD-ult* and *uh-DULT* are common.
- **age**: the cheese-and-meat use (*The cheese is aged for six months*) became its own subsense, and a note gives the American **aging** and British **ageing**.
- **agreement**: a subsense for agreement meaning *saying yes to a plan* (*without your wife's agreement*).
- **aim**: a subsense for words aimed at a person (*Was that joke aimed at me?*), the phrase *aim high*, and a note on *aim to*, *aim for*, and *aim at*.
- **bell**: three phrases, *saved by the bell*, *as clear as a bell*, and the British informal *give someone a bell* (phone someone).
- Short usage notes for **achieve** (used for results you want), **enclose** (*Please find enclosed*; *attach* for email), **motorcycle** (*motorbike*, *bike*), and **ahead** (*ahead of* before a noun).

## What the reviewers found and what I decided

The reviewers raised 27 issues, 15 of them "blocking" (the entry cannot stay marked reviewed until I log a decision). I decided all 27: 18 applied and 9 rejected. The first reviewer was mostly right this time. Four of every five of its points led to a change.

What I accepted:

- **ahead**: the third sense, *in a leading position*, carried *get ahead at work*, which means *become successful*. That is now its own fourth sense. *Ahead of schedule* and *ahead of time* were listed under *later in time*, but they mean *early* and belong to *ahead of*, so I removed them.
- **adult**: the American pronunciation with the stress on the second part is now recorded as a variant, as the note says. The first definition was reworded.
- **agreement**: my new subsense definition was clumsy and has been simplified. The grammar sense no longer limits matching to *number*, since gender and person can match too.
- **oneself**: the emphatic sense said *without help from others*; it now says *rather than leaving it to someone else*.
- **adopt**: the official-acceptance sense lost its "formal" label, since it is normal wording in news and meetings.

What I rejected, and why:

- The claim that *achieve* is also used for bad results. Its examples, *achieve a reduction in crime* and *a low error rate*, are results people want.
- The claim that American *adopt* and *adapt* differ in more than one vowel. Both are stressed on the second part and differ only in that vowel.
- The old spelling *inclose* as a variant of *enclose*. It is no longer current.
- Removing the "euphemistic" label from *adult films* and *adult content*.
- The usual objections to the quoted headword in translators' notes and to the sentence form of summary lines, both house style.
