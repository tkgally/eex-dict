## Back-links

The cross-reference tool (`tools/crossref.py`), run over every entry, adds "back-links". When entry A lists entry B as a synonym, antonym, "compare" word, or member of its word family, the tool adds the mirror link from B back to A. This time it added 25 back-links in 18 entries, almost all of them adjectives from the recent builds (*quick* to *sick*). I checked each by hand. The tool has to guess which sense of the target the link belongs to, and with adjectives that have many senses it often guesses wrong.

Seven were on the wrong sense, and I moved each one to the right sense:

- *quick* ← *quickly* ("at a fast speed"): moved from "taking only a short time" to "moving or acting fast".
- *real* ← *false* as an antonym ("not real, made to look real"): moved to the sense "not a copy or something made up".
- *rich* ← *full* ("strong, rich, and pleasant in taste or sound"): moved from the food sense to "strong, deep, and pleasant".
- *separate* ← *individual*: moved from "not joined or touching" to "different, and not connected with each other".
- *serious* ← *big* ("important or serious"): moved from "very bad or dangerous" to "important and needing careful thought".
- *serious* ← *funny* as an antonym ("making you laugh"): moved to "meaning what you say, and not joking".
- *serious* ← *heavy* ("serious and hard to deal with"): moved from "giving a lot of time and effort" to "important and needing careful thought".

The other fifteen sense links are on the right senses. Examples: *rare* ↔ *frequent*, *sad* ↔ *blue* and *low*, *secret* ↔ *private*, *sick* ↔ *funny* ("slightly ill"). The three new word-family links (*belittle* ← *little*, *real* ← *realize*, *sensitive* ← *sense*) are also right. A second full cross-reference run changed nothing, so the moves hold. The details are in `wiki/notes/crossref-first-sense-backlinks.md`.
