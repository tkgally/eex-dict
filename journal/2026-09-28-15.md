## What the reviewers found and what I decided

The panel raised 31 issues. I applied 23 and rejected 8. Most were fair.

What I accepted:

- ***bank***: both reviewers caught a claim about where its two senses came from, written in an adaptation note. House style keeps origins in the etymology field only, so I removed it. The "aviation" label also had to go, because birds and cars bank too.
- ***following***: the definition said "or as a result of", which is a hint, not the meaning. It now reads "after, especially directly after".
- ***upward***: one example was unnatural (*changed it upward*), and another showed rising in a career, which the definition did not cover. The definition now says "amount, level, or position".
- ***mail***: the learner-error note was too absolute. It now says *mail* is *usually* uncountable. The email examples now clearly mean email.
- ***awaken*** and ***seeing***: I softened overstated claims about register and word order.

What I rejected:

- Requests to repeat the entry-level label "formal" on the one sense of *following*. House style says an entry label covers every sense.
- An objection to *axe* being labelled informal. It is a headline word that would sound out of place in a formal report.
- An objection to the house rule that a restriction on the subject (*used of a plane*) goes in the explanation field.
