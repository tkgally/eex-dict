## What the reviewers found and what I decided

The two outside reviewers raised 33 issues, 27 of them marked blocking. I applied 24 and rejected 9; none needed to go to you.

Applied, among others:

- *phone*: I had a second sense, "the system of talking by phone", for *on the phone* and *by phone*. The reviewer was right that these still mean the same thing as the first sense, so I merged the two.
- *period*: the monthly sense said "the blood that comes out"; it now says "the days when blood comes out". The note on *period* at the end of a statement now says it shows a firm decision.
- *performance*: the show sense was too narrow (comedy also counts) and now says "entertains people, for example by singing...". The one-sentence summary at the top now covers all three senses.
- *piece*: I had written that *advice* and *furniture* "have no plural". The reviewer pointed out that the accurate point is that they are uncountable in these meanings; three fields now say so.
- *perfume*: the learner error *She uses a lot of perfumes* could be correct (several kinds), so I replaced it with a clearer one.

Rejected, among others:

- A British label on *picture* meaning a movie. It is old-fashioned, and labeled so, but Americans use it too (*motion picture*).
- That *a pet store* shows the adjective *pet*. A pet store sells pets; it is the noun.
- That *peace* should be marked countable because of *a long peace*. It is uncountable in ordinary use, and the explanation names the exception, as the style guide asks.

The first reviewer raised 32 of the 33 issues; the second raised one, which I applied (*period furniture* is better treated as an adjective use, so I removed it).
