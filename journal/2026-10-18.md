# 2026-10-18, cycle 1 of the 00:43 UTC run: second review of *peace* to *plastic*

## What this cycle did

This was a **review** cycle: a second reading of entries that had been through the two outside reviewers (the review panel, two language models from other companies) only once. The scheduler (`tools/next_mode.py`) chose review because it was the most overdue kind of work, and the block is always the oldest such entries. This time that meant twenty nouns drafted on 2026-10-06, in alphabetical order: *peace, performance, perfume, period, personality, pet, phone, photograph, picture, piece, pig, pill, pilot, pipe, pity, place, plan, plane, plant, plastic*.

Each entry went to both reviewers again. I read every issue against the entry and either changed the entry (*applied*) or left it as it was (*rejected*, with a logged reason in `reviews/decisions.jsonl`). Nothing was escalated to the owner.

## What the reviewers found and what I decided

reviewer-a raised 38 issues, 28 of them marked blocking (meaning the entry should not stand without a decision). reviewer-b raised 4 and gave a verdict on every field, so its replies were complete. Six entries drew no issues from either reviewer (*personality, phone, photograph, pill, place, plastic*). In all I applied 16 and rejected 26.

Applied:

- ***performance***: the opening summary (the *core idea*, one sentence that says what all the senses share) began *the way someone does something*, which does not describe a show. It now says *the act of doing something in front of other people or as a task*.
- ***pig***: the animal was defined as having *a fat pink or dark body*. Pigs come in many colors, and *fat* reads as a judgment; the definition now says *a large round body*. The core idea had tied the police-officer insult to behaving like a pig; it now just says the word is also used to say rude things about a person.
- ***piece***: the art sense read as if music were *written or painted*; it now says *something that someone has made as music, writing, or art, such as a short tune or a newspaper article*. *Man* stays as a synonym of the game piece, because the *man* entry lists *piece* back, but it now carries a note: only in some games, such as checkers and chess.
- ***plan***: the service sense said such plans are *paid for in regular amounts*, which fits a phone plan but not a savings plan. It now reads *an arrangement in which you pay regular amounts for a service or to save money*. *Plan B* lost its *informal* label: it is used in plain business writing too.
- ***period***: the punctuation sense had an explanation field holding only *British English says full stop*. Explanations are for how a word is used, so the British word moved into the usage note.
- ***perfume***: an explanation that only repeated the countability rule (already in the grammar and the learner-error box) was removed.
- Collocations added: *an organ pipe*, *play a pipe* (`pipe`, music sense); *it's a pity*, *what a pity*, *a great pity* (`pity`, the *shame* sense). `peace`'s *make peace* became *make peace with someone*. `plant`'s *a house plant* (usually one word) became *a potted plant* (reviewer-b).
- ***pipe***: the smoking sense keeps *dried leaves* in its definition, because *tobacco* is not in the defining vocabulary, but an example now names tobacco.

Rejected, with the main reasons:

- *pet project* and *pilot project*: both reviewers called these nouns used before nouns rather than adjectives. The house treats them as adjectives, as learner's dictionaries commonly do, and `pet-adj` and `pilot-adj` are already in the queue. *A pet store* is a store for pets, so it belongs with the animal sense.
- *A sweet perfume* for a flower's smell is a normal countable use, like *a scent*.
- *Make a performance* is not idiomatic; the learner error stays.
- *Plane* is the everyday word in American speech too; the usage note stands.
- The *old-fashioned* labels on *picture* meaning a movie, British *the pictures*, and *for pity's sake* stand; *more's the pity* stays British; *put that in your pipe and smoke it* needs only *informal*.
