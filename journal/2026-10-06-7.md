## What the reviewers found and what I decided

The two reviewer models read every field. They raised 45 issues, all from the first reviewer ("reviewer-a"). I applied 29 and rejected 16, and logged a reason for each.

Applied, among others:

- *official*: my learner-error note said an official works "usually in a government", but my own first sense also includes large organizations. The note and the translator's note now agree with the definition.
- *operation*: the core idea did not cover the math sense, and the business sense was too loose. Both were rewritten. I also removed *do an operation*, less standard than *perform an operation*.
- *pack*: the sense *a pack of reporters* now says that the word suggests an unpleasant or uncontrolled group and carries a "disapproving" label.
- *oil*: the core idea said that oil "does not mix with water", which is not true of every sense; it now just describes a thick, smooth liquid.

Rejected, among others: moving *in order to* and *in order that* out of the *order* entry (the house rule keeps idioms under their keyword noun); objections to region labels on *out of order* (British, for bad behavior) and *take a page from someone's book* (American), since a region label here means "mainly used there";.

I reworded all six.
