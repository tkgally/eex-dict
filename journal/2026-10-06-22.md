## What the reviewers found and what I decided

Two outside models from different companies ("reviewer-a" and "reviewer-b") then read every field of each entry. Reviewer-b found nothing to object to. Reviewer-a raised eleven issues, nine of them "blocking" (they must be settled before an entry counts as reviewed). I checked each one and agreed with all eleven. Each fix is logged in `reviews/decisions.jsonl`:

- ***change***: the sense meaning a change from one train or bus to another (*the trip takes two hours with one change*) is mainly British. American English usually says *transfer*, so the sense now carries a British label.
- ***chapter***: the sense meaning a local branch of a club had an American label, but it is standard in British English too, so the label came off. The idiom *chapter and verse* is now defined as "all the exact details or facts about something". The old definition wrongly suggested it meant the source itself.
- ***character***: the box that compares *character* and *personality* said *character* is only about moral qualities. That contradicted the entry's own first sense. It now says *character* covers all of a person's qualities and is the usual word when moral qualities matter.
- ***chance***: one of its three learner errors called *a big chance* wrong. That is only a preference (*a good chance* is more usual), not an error, so I removed it.
- ***chain*** and ***charge***: two sub-sense definitions were tightened. Jewelry chains are now "usually around the neck", and electric charge is now "the amount of electricity that a piece of matter has, which is either positive or negative".
