*Accepted:*

- *worry*: the usage note had said people do not say *I have a worry*, which is too strong when there is one particular concern. It now says the verb is more common, and that the noun is used for a particular concern.
- *writer*: the usage note now allows *writer* for the person who wrote a particular book, as well as *author*.
- *word* sense 1 no longer says a word is defined by spaces around it. *yard*'s note on British use now includes work areas.

*Rejected:*

- Reviewer-a said a "plural only" sense for *years* (*I haven't seen him in years*) conflicts with the regular plural. House practice marks a sense used only in the plural this way.
- Five smaller objections did not hold up.

**Back-links.** One back-link was added: *promise* sense 1 now names *word* as a synonym. It is on the right sense.
