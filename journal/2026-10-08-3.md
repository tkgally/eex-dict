## Back-links

When entry A names entry B as a synonym, an opposite, a word to compare, or a family member, a tool adds the reverse link to B (a "back-link"). The full run added 19, most of them mirrors pointing at the twelve *s* nouns written in the previous cycle and the twelve before that. A back-link to a single sense is placed by the tool's guess at the best-matching sense, and it is often wrong, so each lint checks every one by hand. Five of the sixteen sense-level back-links were on the wrong sense, and I moved them:

- *show*: the synonym *display* (a public event where things are shown) moved from the stage-performance sense to the event sense (*a flower show*).
- *side*: the synonym *face* (one surface of something, such as a mountain) moved from "left or right" to "surface".
- *sign*: the synonym *mark* (something that shows a quality is present) moved from the written-symbol sense to the evidence sense (*a sign of rain*).
- *sheet*: "compare *page*" moved from the bed sheet to the sheet of paper.
- *shame*: the opposite *pride* (a feeling of pleasure in what you have done) moved from "loss of respect" to the feeling sense.

The other eleven were right. The tool also reported that *second* and *minute* named each other under different kinds of link. Both entries were right sense by sense (they are synonyms for "a very short time"), but *minute* also said "compare *second*" for the unit of time and *second* did not say the same back. I added "compare *minute*" to the time-unit sense of *second*, and a fresh full run then reported nothing.
