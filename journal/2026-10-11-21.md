## What this cycle did

A **lint cycle** is the dictionary's housekeeping pass. It makes no new entries and no paid calls. It runs the mechanical checks over everything, then a judgmental read of the knowledge base (the `wiki/` pages that hold the project's rules and notes) for stale or contradictory statements. The scheduler (`tools/next_mode.py`) forced a lint because five cycles had passed since the last one.

Before the lint itself, the pre-flight checks found no open pull requests left behind by earlier runs. Three old branches are fully merged and already have a line in the owner's question file asking for them to be deleted, since this tool set cannot delete branches. The inbox was empty.

The mechanical checks:

- **Size caps** (`tools/check_caps.py`): all respected.
- **Links** between the project's pages (`tools/check_links.py`): all 500 pages resolve.
- **Cross-references** (`tools/crossref.py --all --apply`): when entry A names entry B as a synonym, opposite, or related word, B should name A back. The tool added fifteen such return links on ten entries, almost all of them the adjectives built earlier today. I checked each by hand, because the tool sometimes attaches a link to the wrong sense of the target word. Fourteen were right: for example, *park* now appears beside the *public garden* sense of *garden*, and *cheap* as the opposite of *generous*. One was wrong: *large* got *generous* on its first sense, *big in size*, but *generous* means large only in the sense of *more than is needed* (*a generous helping*). I moved it by hand to the second sense of *large*, *more than usual in amount or number*.
- **A mismatch between two entries.** The tool also reported, without changing anything, that *ashamed* listed *guilty* as a synonym, while *guilty* listed *ashamed* both as a synonym and as a word to compare. A word cannot be both on the same sense, and both entries already carry a short note explaining how *ashamed* and *guilty* differ (guilt is about having done wrong; shame is about how you see yourself, often in front of others). Words that need such a note are better marked "compare" than "synonym", so both entries now list each other under "compare" only.
- **Defining vocabulary** (`tools/lint_vocab.py --all --queue`): the rule is that every word in a definition is either in the defining word list or has its own entry. It found no violations in all 2,134 entries and queued nothing new.
- **Claim files** (`tools/claim.py --prune`): three old claim files (records of which words a past cycle had taken) were removed. All their words are done.
