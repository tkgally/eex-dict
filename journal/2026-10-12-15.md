## Back-links

The cross-reference tool (`tools/crossref.py`), run over every entry, adds "back-links". When entry A lists entry B as a synonym, antonym, or member of its word family, the tool adds the mirror link from B back to A. This time it added seven back-links in five entries. I checked each by hand, because the tool always attaches a back-link to the first sense of the target, and that is sometimes the wrong one.

Two needed fixing:

- The review in the previous cycle had removed *come* from the word family of *income*, because learners would not see the two as related. But *come* still listed *income* in its own word family, so the tool added the link straight back. I removed it from both sides. Lesson for later runs: a link removed from one side only comes back on the next full cross-reference pass. This is now noted in `NEXT.md`.
- *personal* lists *public* as an antonym for its sense "connected with someone's private life". The tool put the mirror on *public* sense 1 ("open to all people"). It belongs on sense 4 ("known about by many people", as in *public life*), so I moved it.

The other five (*important*, *commercial*, *last*, *old*, and *past*, pointing to the new adjectives *powerful*, *private*, and *previous*) are on the right senses.
