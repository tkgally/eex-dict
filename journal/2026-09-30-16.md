## What the reviewers found and what I decided

Two outside language models, reviewer-a and reviewer-b, read every field of every entry. They raised 19 issues, 11 of them marked blocking. I read each one against the entry and decided it myself: 9 applied, 10 rejected, none sent on to the owner.

Applied:

- *divide and conquer*: the definition said the other side was kept "split into small groups". It now reads "keep power over a group by making its members argue with each other, so that they cannot act together". Its examples were replaced. One of them, *A good way to solve a big problem is to divide and conquer*, showed a different meaning (breaking a problem into parts) that the definition did not cover.
- *divide*, the sense about people disagreeing: the collocations are now in the passive (*be bitterly divided*, *be sharply divided*), which is how the word is really used.
- *draw*: the example *I was drawn to her from the moment we met* was coded as an active pattern; the code was removed and the example keeps its note, *passive*.
- *not dream of doing something*: it said you would not do it "because you think it would be wrong". It now also covers things that just do not seem right for you, and it starts with the base form of the verb, as the house style requires.
- *drive*: *run* as a synonym for driving someone somewhere now carries the note "informal, for a short trip".

Rejected, with reasons logged:

- That *The new restaurant did not disappoint*, with no object, is unnatural. It is ordinary English.
- That, after a negative, *doubt* is naturally followed by *whether*. *I don't doubt that* is the normal form.
- That the American transcription of *dream* needs a length mark, and that adaptation notes may not open with the headword in quotation marks. Both are settled house practice (the "fences" in `NEXT.md`).
- That *drink* in the alcohol sense should be defined with the word *alcohol*. The explanation already says it, and *alcohol* is outside the defining vocabulary.
- Four objections about pattern codes that the closed list does not support.
