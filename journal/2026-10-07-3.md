## What the reviewers found and what I decided

The two outside reviewer models (reviewer-a and reviewer-b) raised 32 issues: 28 from reviewer-a and 4 from reviewer-b. I applied 24 and rejected 8, each with a logged reason. Three entries (*city*, *clay*, *client*) drew no issues at all.

The main fixes:

- **church**, sense 3, had been defined as any religious group. It now says a Christian group, or all Christians together. The *church and state* phrase was reworded to match.
- **citizen** had *resident* listed as a synonym, which contradicted its own new usage note. *Resident* now appears only as a word to compare, with a short note. The town sense now mentions rights and duties, and *senior citizen* is now defined by age, not by whether the person has stopped working.
- **class**, sense 5, now covers the travel classes, as in *first class* and *economy class*. Its "style" sense lost an informal label that the reviewer rightly said it does not need.
- **chest** had two separate notes comparing it with *breast*. They are merged into one.
- **circle** had *ring* as a synonym of the geometric shape. It was moved to the "round group" sense. The note on theater seats now says American theaters use *balcony* or *mezzanine*.

The rejections:

- **The *chest* translation note.** It warns that many languages use separate words for the body part and the box. The reviewer read it as a claim about the history of English.
- **The *chicken* learner error *I ate a chicken for lunch*.** It stays, because the meant reading is the meat.
- ***The church* as uncountable.** The reviewer called the institutional use uncountable, but *the church* is a countable noun used with *the*.
- **A "mainly American" label on graduating *class*.** It stays, under the house rule that a region label means a word is mainly used there, not only there.
- **Three issues on *claim*.**
  - A formal label for *stake a claim*: the phrase is neutral.
  - A doubt about the soup-complaint learner error: it is a real mistake in that context.
  - One reviewer-b comment that withdrew itself halfway through.
