# 2026-10-19, cycle 3 of the 00:43 UTC run: second review of *protection* to *question*, and ten more

## What this cycle did

This was a **review** cycle: a second reading of entries that had been through the two outside reviewers (the review panel, two language models from other companies) only once. The scheduler (`tools/next_mode.py`) chose review because it was the most overdue kind of work, and the block is always the oldest such entries. This time the block was twenty entries drafted on 2026-10-07: the nouns *protection, protest, public, punishment, purpose, purse, quality, quarter, queen, question*; the verbs *cash, center, chain, chair, circle, cloud, coat, code*; and the nouns *education* and *instruction*.

Each entry went to both reviewers again. I read every issue against the entry and either changed the entry (*applied*) or left it as it was (*rejected*, with a logged reason in `reviews/decisions.jsonl`). Nothing was escalated to the owner.

## What the reviewers found and what I decided

reviewer-a raised 44 issues, 36 of them blocking (meaning the entry should not stand without a decision); reviewer-b raised 8. Three entries drew no issues (*quality, chair, cloud*). In all I applied 33 and rejected 19.

Applied:

- ***public***: both reviewers objected to the phrase *go public*, whose definition joined two meanings with a colon and *or*. It now reads *make something known to everyone that was secret before*, and the company meaning (offering shares for anyone to buy) is in the explanation.
- ***queen***: the opening summary (the *core idea*) said the chess piece, the card, and the insect *take their name from* a queen. That is a claim about word history, which house style allows only in the etymology field, so it now simply lists the uses. The ruler sense now says *usually because she was born into a royal family*, and *a drama queen* is marked as a phrase named as a phrase.
- ***quarter***: the time sense claimed that American English says *a quarter of six*; it now says that many Americans say *a quarter after* and some say *a quarter of*. The translator's note was softened the same way.
- ***punishment***, ***instruction***, ***protest***, ***protection***: notes that were too absolute were softened. A judge *usually* gives a *sentence*, which is one kind of punishment; the plural *instructions* is usual for a set of steps, but one step can be *an instruction*; many Americans stress the verb *protest* on the first part; and the grammar note on *protection* now separates *protection of children* (who is protected) from *protection for workers* (who it is meant for). A learner error on *protest* that no learner would make (*against to*) was replaced with a real one (*a protest to the new law*).
- ***cash*** (verb): *a check or similar paper* became *a check or another written order to pay*, and the gloss of *cash out* now says *take money out of an account, an investment, or a game*.
- ***chain*** (verb): the code *usually passive* was wrong (*She chained her bike to the fence*) and is gone; *chain something together*, which means link, was removed from the *fasten* sense.
- ***education***: the note that *a good education* takes *a* sat on the wrong sense; it moved to sense 2 (*knowledge you gain*), which now explains when the noun is countable. Two old notes still used plain quotation marks instead of the house marks; I fixed them.
- Smaller fixes: *in question* now means *being talked about or considered*; `circle-v` got a shorter signpost and collocations; `coat-v` and `code-v` got a more natural example and correct collocation types; `purse-n` and `purpose-n` got small note fixes.

Rejected, with the main reasons:

- Two reviewer requests would have put *dollar* (`quarter`) and *chess* (`queen`) into definitions. Neither word is in the defining vocabulary (the fixed list of plain words definitions are written in), and neither has an entry yet, so the explanations keep carrying them.
- `center-v`: reviewer-a said the *main subject* definition does not fit *The story centers on a family*; it does, and only the explanation needed sharpening.
- *Answer* stays as the opposite of *question*; *the public* in its people-in-general sense always takes *the* (*public bodies* is the adjective); *queen of something* is said of women, and *drama queen* has its own explanation.
