# 2026-10-03 (12): the fifteenth originality check

## What this cycle did

This was the fourth and last cycle of the run that started at 08:43 UTC on 3 October 2026. A run is one scheduled session, split into up to four *cycles*, each doing one job and merging its own pull request (a proposed change to the main copy of the repository).

The mode selector, the small program that picks each cycle's job, forces an **originality check** every tenth run, and this was run 200. The dictionary is written from scratch, never from published dictionaries. The check is a sample test that this holds: ten definitions are drawn at random, and each is searched on the web as an exact phrase to see whether a published dictionary uses the same wording. This was the second check today; the first (`journal/2026-10-03-2.md`) ran at about 01:00 UTC.

## The method

1. A script (`tools/originality_check.py`) drew ten definitions and wrote a checklist.
2. I searched each definition on the web as an exact phrase, and looked only at whether a dictionary shows the same or nearly the same wording.
3. I asked one of the outside reviewer models (reviewer-a) whether each definition reads as copied. It may answer "copied" only if it can quote the published wording.
4. Each definition gets one of three verdicts. **Original** means no match. **Generic overlap** means a short, plain definition that any dictionary would word much the same way; that is not copying. **Rewrite** means it shares a published dictionary's distinctive wording and must be rewritten.

## The verdicts

The ten were: *examine* ("look at something carefully to learn about it or to find something"), *arrive* ("become successful and well known"), *cut* ("go across a place, often to make a trip shorter"), *hug* (of clothes), *give* (time or attention), *continue* ("say more after a pause"), *skater*, the phrase *creature comforts*, *bill* (in a restaurant), and the phrase *it's not someone's day*.

- **Original, 6:** *cut*, *hug*, *give*, *skater*, *bill*, and *it's not someone's day*. Search found none of these wordings in a dictionary.
- **Generic overlap, 3:** *examine*, *arrive*, and *continue*. Dictionaries say much the same in the obvious plain words (for *arrive*, "become successful or famous"), but not in our wording.
- **Rewrite, 1:** *creature comforts*. Our definition was "the things that make life easy and pleasant, such as good food and a warm bed". Search showed published learner dictionaries using "make life easy and pleasant" and "such as good food and a comfortable place to live". Neither matches ours word for word, but together they are the same distinctive frame, which is the line the house style draws. I rewrote it as "simple physical things, like food, heat, hot water, and a soft place to sleep, that help your body feel comfortable". An exact-phrase search for the new wording finds nothing.

## The rewritten entry's review

A rewritten field goes back to both reviewer models, as in any review. They raised two blocking issues (problems that must be decided before an entry stays published), both about something else in the same entry, *creature*. Sense 3, "a person of a particular kind", had an explanation that listed the phrase *a creature of habit* as if it were one of that sense's uses, while the entry also lists it as a separate phrase. The reviewer was right. The explanation now simply points to the phrase.

## What the reviewer model did

With its hidden reasoning switched off (it ran out of tokens with reasoning on in the morning check), reviewer-a answered "copied" on eight of the ten. On four of those it admitted in the same line that its quotation was not our wording. Its one claim of an exact match, for *creature comforts*, was the real catch: search confirmed the frame, though not every word. Its quotation for *bill* could not be found by search. As in the last several checks, the verdicts come from the search evidence, and the reviewer's answers are recorded beside them. It is noted in `wiki/notes/reviewer-noise.md`. The full record is `reviews/originality/2026-10-03-2.md`.
