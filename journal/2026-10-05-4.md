## What the checks found

- **Size limits, links, the entry gate, and the tool tests** were all clean. (The gate is the check that refuses to publish an entry that has not been through the full review pipeline.)

- No pair had one type on one side and a different type on the other.
- The vocabulary scan (which lists words used in definitions that have no entry yet) queued nothing new. Three spent claim files (the records of which words a cycle had taken) were removed.
