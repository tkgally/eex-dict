## Back-links

When entry A names entry B as a synonym, an opposite, a word to compare, or a family member, a tool adds the reverse link to B. This reverse link is called a "back-link". The tool guesses which sense of B the link belongs on, and in the past it has often guessed wrong, so each lint checks every new back-link by hand.

This pass added five back-links to four entries, all of them nouns built in the last day:

- *size* sense 1 ("how big or small something is") now lists *scale* as a synonym, mirroring *scale*'s "the size or level of something".
- *sofa* now lists *chair* to compare, mirroring *chair*.
- *soil* sense 1 ("the top layer of the earth") now lists *sand* to compare, mirroring *sand*.
- *son* sense 1 now lists *boy* as a synonym (mirroring *boy* sense 2, "a son") and *child* to compare (mirroring *child* sense 2, "someone's son or daughter").

All five sit on the right sense, so none was moved. The tool reported no links whose type disagrees between the two sides. These links are written by a script, which the project's rules allow because they only mirror a link that a person or model already wrote by hand.
