# 2026-09-28 — Review: fifteen verbs, *read* to *refuse* and *push* to *reach*

## What this run did

This was the first cycle of the scheduled run that began at 00:44 UTC. A "cycle" is one unit of work, checked, recorded, and merged on its own; a run stops after at most four. Before starting, I checked for unfinished work from earlier runs: there were no open pull requests and no leftover branches, and the owner's inbox was empty.

The mode selector (the script that decides what each cycle does) chose **review**: a second reading of entries that have passed the reviewer panel only once. The block was fifteen verbs written late on 2026-09-26, when long runs were producing thinner entries. The first eight were *read, realize, recognize, recover, reduce, refer, reflect,* and *refuse*. The other seven were *push, question, quit, race, rain, raise,* and *reach*.

I read each entry before sending it to the panel and added what was missing:

- **read**: a new sense, *understand or explain something in a particular way* (*I read her silence as a yes*; *How do you read the situation?*), and a pointer to the phrasal verb *read out*, which is now in the queue.
- **reach**: a new sense, *go as far as a particular place, point, or number of people* (*Her hair reached her waist*; *The show reaches millions of viewers*).
- **push**: a new sense, *try hard to make people buy, use, or accept something*, with a subsense for selling illegal drugs (labelled informal), and the phrase *push it* (*You can stay up until ten, but don't push it*).
- **raise**: a subsense for keeping animals or growing crops (*They raise sheep and goats*).
- **refuse**: a learner error for the common confusion with *deny* (*He refused that he had taken the money*).
- **quit**: *quitter* added to its word family.
- **reflect**: the fifth sense's signpost (the short label that helps a reader find a sense) said "give an opinion", which was wrong; it now says "good or bad image".

## What the reviewers found and what I decided

The two reviewer models raised 41 issues, 22 of them "blocking" (the entry cannot stay marked reviewed until a decision is logged). I decided all 41: 20 applied and 21 rejected. Each decision is in `reviews/decisions.jsonl`. One reviewer's reply on *read* could not be parsed the first time; I re-ran that one review and it came back clean.

Changes I accepted:

- **race**: *He used to race cars* does not fit the definition "compete with someone to see who is the fastest". Racing cars and horses is now its own subsense.
- **read**: *He read the fear in her face* belonged with the new "understand in a way" sense, not with reading maps and music, so the example and its collocation moved there.
- **reduce**: the cooking sense said "boil"; a sauce can also reduce by slow cooking, so it now says "cook a liquid until there is less of it".
- **realize**: the entry said the "understand" sense is never used in continuous tenses. *I'm only now realizing how lucky I was* is natural English, so the marker is gone.
- **recognize** and **refuse**: their one-sentence summaries at the top of the entry were reworded. *refuse*'s said "to say no: to doing something", which was not grammatical.
- **question** and **realize**: two notes for translators overstated their claims and were softened.
- **quit**: *He's thinking of quitting* did not show that a job was meant; it is now *I've had enough of this place. I quit!*
- **reach**: a note claimed *get* always needs *to* before a place; *get home* shows it does not.

What I rejected, and why:

- The first reviewer said *quitted* is not mainly British. It survives mostly in British English, so the note stands.
- It objected to *know* as a synonym of *recognize*, but the *know* entry already has that sense (*I'd know her voice anywhere*).
- It said "understand what they show" is too narrow for reading music or handwriting. Reading those is exactly understanding what marks show.
- The second reviewer again objected, ten times, to the quoted headword at the start of translators' notes. This is a settled house convention.
