## What this cycle did

The scheduler (`tools/next_mode.py`, the script that picks each cycle's job) chose **lint**, a housekeeping pass over the whole project. It runs the mechanical checks over everything, then reads the knowledge base (the `wiki/` folder of rules, decisions, and notes) for contradictions, missing pages, and stale claims.

- **Links** (`tools/check_links.py`, which checks that every link inside the knowledge base points to a real file): all resolve.
- **Unit tests** for the tools: all pass.
- **Back-links** (`tools/crossref.py --all --apply`). When entry A names entry B as a synonym, an opposite, a word to compare, or a family member, B should name A too, and this tool adds the missing half. It added six: *brain* as a synonym on *intelligence*, *come* in the word family of *income*, *cut* and *gain* on *increase*, *harm* on *injury*, the preposition *inside* in the family of the noun, and *per* on the second sense of *according to*. The tool sometimes picks the wrong sense, so I read each one; all six landed on the right sense. It found no pairs of entries that disagree about the kind of link.
- **Words in definitions** (`tools/lint_vocab.py --all --queue`, which finds words used in definitions that are neither in the defining vocabulary nor entries, and queues them): no new problems beyond those already queued.

## Reviewer precision

Every objection from the two outside reviewer models is logged with my decision, so we can measure how often each reviewer is right in each kind of objection ("precision"). The rule is that a kind of objection right less than 30 percent of the time, over twenty or more decisions, is switched off for that reviewer. Six such pairs are already off. Two more have been under the line for days but are held for the owner. A safety check refused the switch-off edit on 2026-10-01, and the question is in `reviews/needs_curator.txt`. They are reviewer-a's pronunciation objections (27 percent, mostly asking for a length mark that American transcriptions in this dictionary leave out by house rule; one more on *tube* this run) and reviewer-b's spelling-and-format objections (28 percent, mostly against a house style for quoting the headword in notes). No other pair is under the line. The figures are in `wiki/notes/reviewer-precision.md`.
