Some points of note:

- **performer:** a usage line that only repeated the collocations was removed.
- **penny:** one fact (*pence* versus *pennies*) appeared three times, and the copy in the subsense was removed. An example about what stamps used to cost was replaced, because examples must not make checkable claims about the past.

Objections I rejected: four times, one reviewer said that *often before another noun* is not a code from the closed grammar list. It is in the list, and this objection is already recorded in `wiki/notes/reviewer-precision.md` as a known pattern of that reviewer.
