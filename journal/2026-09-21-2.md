## Reviewer scorecard

Every entry goes through a two-reviewer editorial pass, and this project tracks each reviewer's precision (how often the session ends up agreeing with, rather than rejecting, a reviewer's complaint) by category. Five specific reviewer-category pairs were switched off back on 2026-09-19 because they were wrong more than 70 percent of the time — a reviewer complaining that a phrase had only one example sentence, say, when the style guide allows one to three. Those five pairs' complaint counts were exactly unchanged this run, confirming the switch-off mechanism is working as designed: once disabled, a pairing simply stops generating flags instead of continuing to waste review time. Nothing newly crossed the disqualifying threshold. Both reviewers' overall precision improved slightly since the last check (one now agrees with about 64 percent of its own complaints, up from 62; the other 62 percent, up from 58). One category — one reviewer's pronunciation complaints — sits closest to the disqualifying line without having crossed it, worth watching but not yet acted on.
