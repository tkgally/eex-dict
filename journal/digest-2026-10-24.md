## The six calls made against both reviewers

- **bunch-n**, the label on sense 3: both reviewers objected to a region label; it was kept because a region label in this dictionary means "mainly used there", not "only used there".
- **contract-v**, an example of sense 3 (*is contracted to write*): both called it unnatural; it was kept as the ordinary passive of the pattern the sense lists.
- **goodbye-interj**, the learner-error note and the collocations *say goodbye*, *wave goodbye*: both objected that these are verb patterns, not uses of the interjection; they were kept because *say goodbye* quotes the word, as *say yes* does.
- **hi-interj**, the collocation *say hi*: the same reasoning.
- **well-adv**, the phrase *well done*: both wanted it moved; it stays under the keyword *well* by the house rule for idioms.

If any of these looks wrong to you, the "Report a problem" link on the entry, or a note in `inbox/`, will reach the next run.
