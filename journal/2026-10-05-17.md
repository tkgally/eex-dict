A lint pass is the regular health check of the whole project. Its mechanical part runs the checking tools over every dictionary entry and every page of the knowledge base. Its judgmental part reads the knowledge base (the `wiki/` pages that hold the rules and the record of decisions) and the owner-facing files for contradictions, gaps, and out-of-date claims.
