## What the reviewers found and what I decided

The two outside reviewers raised 40 issues: 38 from reviewer-a, 2 from reviewer-b. 36 were blocking. I applied 32 and rejected 8. reviewer-b's first reply on *store* was cut off, so it was re-run and came back complete.

Applied:

- *stone*: the fruit-seed sense is labeled British, since American English says *pit*. The jewel sense now gives *gem* as its synonym instead of *jewel*. A learner error now teaches the plural *twelve stone*; the old one changed the measurement instead of correcting the grammar.
- *store*: the storage-room sense is labeled British. The note on American *store* and British *shop* is softer: British English does use *store*, especially for large shops.
- *stop*: a station is no longer described as always being a building.
- *story*: *history* can also mean a description or study of the past, not only the past itself. The sense "an explanation that is not true" now adds "told to trick someone".
- *steam*: the example about steam on a window was replaced with steam coming out of a pan; the reviewer was right that a misted window is not really steam. An adaptation note that explained where the *steam* idioms came from was removed. The house rule keeps claims about a word's origin only in the etymology field.
- *stranger*: the claim that *stranger* "means only" a person you do not know conflicted with its second sense (a person new to a place), so it was reworded.

Rejected: five flags from reviewer-a saying that *strategy* has no uncountable sense. *Chess is a game of strategy* and *military strategy* have no article, which shows the uncountable use. Also rejected: the claim that *a heavy storm* is not typical; the objection to *You need a strong stomach to work in a hospital*; and reviewer-b's call for verb definitions that start with *to*, which goes against house style.

Cross-references: the tool reported two type mismatches, where one entry called the other a synonym but the other called it a "compare" word. I settled both on "compare" (*step*/*stage*, *store*/*shop*). Five new back-links were checked; one, on *account*, landed on the bank sense and was moved by hand to the "description of an event" sense.
