# 2026-09-27 (2) — Lint: the eighteenth housekeeping pass

## What this run did

This was the second cycle of the scheduled run that began at 18:44 UTC. A "cycle" is one unit of work that is checked, recorded, and merged on its own. The mode selector (`tools/next_mode.py`) forced a **lint** pass because five runs had gone by since the last one. A lint pass is housekeeping. Scripts check the whole dictionary and its notes. Then I read their output and the project's own pages for things a script cannot judge.

## What was checked, and what it found

- **Word caps and links.** Every page is within its word limit, and every relative link resolves.
- **Cross-references.** When entry A lists entry B as a synonym or a word to compare, B should list A back. `tools/crossref.py` adds those mirror links, but it has to guess which sense of B to attach them to, and it guesses wrong about one time in five. So every lint pass checks each one by hand. This pass it wrote six. Five were right: *deliver/send*, *divide/share*, *fix/set*, *govern/rule*, and *lay/set*. The sixth attached *refer* (as in *refer a patient to a specialist*) to the mail sense of *send*. I moved it to the sense *make someone go*, where it belongs.
- **Vocabulary queue.** A second script finds words used in definitions that have no entry yet and adds them to the list of words to write. It found nothing new.
- **Stale claims.** Two old claim files were removed. A claim file is how a run reserves headwords so that two runs do not write the same word.
- **Reviewer precision.** Each finding from the two reviewer models is either applied or rejected. Precision is the share that gets applied. For each kind of finding, any reviewer below 30 percent over twenty or more decisions stops being asked about that kind. No new kind crossed the line this time; five were switched off earlier and stay off. Two kinds are close:
  - The first reviewer's pronunciation findings: 32 percent over 112 decisions.
  - Its inflection findings (the forms of a word, such as *ran* and *run*): 6 percent, but over only 16 decisions, four short of the twenty needed.
  The figures are in `wiki/notes/reviewer-precision.md`.
- **The curator list.** `reviews/needs_curator.txt` is the list of items for you. Two lines asked for old branches to be deleted. Both branches are already gone, so I closed those lines.
- **Index, open questions, decisions.** The wiki index lists every page. The open questions still carry their working assumptions, and nothing contradicts the decision pages.
