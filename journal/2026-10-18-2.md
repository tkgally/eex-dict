# 2026-10-18, cycle 2 of the 00:43 UTC run: the seventy-seventh lint pass

## What this cycle did

This was a **lint** cycle: a housekeeping pass over the whole dictionary and its knowledge base (the `wiki/` folder of rules and notes), with no new entries and no paid model calls. The scheduler (`tools/next_mode.py`) forces one every five cycles; this was the fifth since the last. A lint pass has two halves: mechanical checks run by scripts, and a reading pass where I check that the wiki pages agree with each other.

## The mechanical checks

- **Size caps** (`tools/check_caps.py`) on the rule files and the wiki: all respected.
- **Links** between wiki and project pages (`tools/check_links.py`): all resolve.
- **Vocabulary queue** (`tools/lint_vocab.py --all --queue`): words used in definitions that are outside the plain defining vocabulary and have no entry yet. Nothing new was queued.
- **Claim files** (`tools/claim.py --prune`): three spent claim files, records of which words earlier cycles reserved, were removed.
- **Back-links** (`tools/crossref.py --all --apply`). Cross-references in this dictionary are symmetric: if entry A lists B as a synonym, B should list A. The script finds one-sided links and adds the missing half. It proposed fourteen this time, and I checked every one by hand, because the script always attaches the new link to the target's first sense, which is sometimes the wrong one.

## What I found in the back-links

Four of the fourteen would have quietly undone decisions from yesterday's review of *paper* to *payment*. That review removed *fire* as a synonym of *passion*, *garden* as a synonym of *park*, and *slice* and *unit* as synonyms of *part*, because none of them can usually replace the word. But it removed them on one side only. The other entries still pointed back (*fire*'s *strong feeling* sense listed *passion*, and so on), so the script saw one-sided links and put the removed synonyms back.

I reverted those four and fixed the other side by hand: *passion* is gone from `fire-n`, and *part* from `slice-n` and `unit-n`. `garden-n` is a special case: its *public park* sense really is close to *park*, so instead of a synonym it now has a *compare* link with a short note (a park is mainly grass and trees; public gardens are planted mainly with flowers), and the script added the matching compare link to `park-n`. To stop this happening again, `NEXT.md` now says that a review which removes a synonym must remove the other half in the same cycle.

One more back-link landed on the wrong sense: *so* (meaning *also*) was attached to `too-adv`'s fourth sense (*answering a negative*). I moved it to the second sense, *also*. The other nine are on the right sense: word-family links (*duck*, *ease*, *dull*, *today*, *totally*) and comparisons such as *soon* with *early* and *quickly*, and *then* with *than*.
