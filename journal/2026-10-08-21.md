## Back-links

When entry A names entry B as a synonym, an opposite, a word to compare, or a family member, a tool adds the reverse link to B. This is a "back-link". The tool guesses which sense of B the link belongs on, and it has often guessed wrong, so every lint checks each new back-link by hand.

This pass added five back-links to three entries written today:

- *supply* sense 1 (an amount available) now lists *bank* as a synonym, mirroring *bank*'s sense "a supply of something, such as blood, kept for use". Right sense.
- *strength* sense 1 (the body's physical power) now lists *energy* and *force*. Both mirror senses about physical strength. Right sense.
- *strength* also got *power*, but the tool put it on sense 1. The *power* sense it mirrors is about the force of things, as in *the power of the waves*. That belongs on *strength* sense 4, "how great or powerful something is, for example a wind". **I moved it.**
- *stuff* sense 1 (things in general) now lists *shit* as a synonym, mirroring the vulgar entry's sense "things of any kind". The link is correct, but a learner reading *stuff* would see a vulgar word with no warning. **I added a short note by hand**: "a vulgar word; do not use it in polite speech". Notes on cross-references are written by hand under the project's rules, never by a script.

The tool reported no type mismatches (where one entry calls the other a synonym and the other calls it a "compare" word).
